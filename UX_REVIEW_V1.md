# UX Review V1

Reviewed: 2026-09-23. Baseline: PROJECT.md, SOT.md, DECISIONS.md, ROADMAP.md, UX.md and the owner's V1 mission. Repository baseline 075e2f04dc60befc5ad07d63b090c1a446907516. This is a requirements and workflow audit: there was no implemented application to visually audit at baseline.

## Authority and design read

A Thai employee self-service and finance operations product, not a marketing site. Quiet Enterprise / Modern Editorial: warm neutral canvas, restrained Infinity blue, readable Thai sans serif, compact desktop working tables and touch-friendly mobile forms. Astra owns workflows, accessibility and financial correctness. Installed design-taste-frontend is used only for visual critique. Its marketing-specific hero, animation, imagery and low-density prescriptions do not apply. Design variance 3, motion intensity 2, mobile density 5 and finance density 8.

## Existing strengths retained

The blueprint correctly separates payroll OT from expense reimbursement, managerial approval from finance verification, and digital evidence from original paper receipts. Trip is a parent transaction; Entertainment remains within Expense. Entra-only identity, PostgreSQL transactions, a modular monolith and Docker Compose are proportionate to this organization. Phone-first employees and desktop-first Finance are distinct working modes, not two unrelated products. These decisions remain binding.

## Findings and corrections

| ID | Finding / risk | Required correction | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UX-01 | The declared headcount is 11, but the listed roles total 10 (Sales 2, Engineering 6, Finance 2). | Do not invent the eleventh employee or real names. Model an extensible roster; seed synthetic test identities only. Production roster mapping is an onboarding gate. | No production employee created from an assumption; arbitrary roster size supported. |
| UX-02 | A single status hides payment and receipt exceptions. | Show four independent dimensions: managerial approval, finance verification, payment, digital/original evidence. Paid does not imply original received. | A paid claim remains visible in Original Receipts Outstanding. |
| UX-03 | Head self-skip can be mistaken for self-approval or automatic payment. | Record an immutable SYSTEM_SKIPPED managerial action with policy reference. Finance review still required for financial claims and cannot be done by the claimant. | Owner request skips only the managerial step; Finance owner remains blocked from self-verification/payment. |
| UX-04 | Finance/Admin role overlap invites a hidden override. | Enforce conflict-of-interest in domain services and transaction queries, not only disabled buttons. No administrator bypass. A batch containing the operator's own claim is blocked, with the affected item identified. | Negative API and batch tests, not only screenshots. |
| UX-05 | The cutoff statement lacks an instant, timezone and holiday calendar. | One calendar service owns Asia/Bangkok dates. Show the exclusive cutoff and payroll month on preview and detail. Preserve the applied calendar/policy snapshot. Non-working cutoffs are advanced to the operational boundary defined in the locked policy contract. | Tests at both sides of cutoff, weekends, configured holidays and year rollover. |
| UX-06 | Integer OT hours are easily replaced by a generic time-range form. | Use policy-generated category rows, whole-hour numeric input and a calculated money preview. No start/end time or minutes fields. Separate app Head role from legal OT eligibility classification. | Fractional-hour requests rejected by both UI and server; no manually supplied amount trusted. |
| UX-07 | Leave entitlement, paid leave cap and calendar-day counting differ. | Model these separately. Full-day units do not mean every leave type excludes weekends. Multi-year or policy-boundary requests have explicit line-level policy allocation, or an actionable split request error. Never silently rerate an approved request. | Effective-date, whole-day, overlap, balance and cancellation tests. |
| UX-08 | The old legal baseline may omit recent rights. | Verify and version statutory references before enabling formulas. The 2025 Labour Protection Act amendment 9 changes maternity and family leave; retain ordinary statutory categories rather than offering annual leave alone. | Legal reference register with primary sources and effective dates; unverified rules cannot become an enabled production policy. |
| UX-09 | Returned requests can overwrite the history previously approved. | Correction creates a new request revision; prior snapshot and actions remain immutable. Resubmission starts a new review round. Paid/exported records are read-only; adjustments are separate linked records. | Stale revision gets 409; history shows each round and correction reason. |
| UX-10 | Mobile forms risk losing work on navigation or network failure. | Progressive sections, visible server-draft save state and unsaved-change warning. Offline means read-only shell plus explicit inability to submit; do not cache HR/receipt data in a service worker or silently queue financial commands. | Offline/error/retry screens; idempotent submit; no private response in Cache Storage. |
| UX-11 | Receipt upload is not equivalent to verified safe evidence. | Camera or file picker first, filename/type/size/progress/retry visible. Private storage and authorized download, content validation and metadata hash. Original state is independent. Never expose a permanent public receipt URL. | Unauthorized download and unsafe upload tests; paid-with-digital happy path. |
| UX-12 | Mileage deductions can be incorrectly applied once per claim or to office legs. | Show every leg, origin class, distance source, normal commute deduction, eligible distance and rate. Clamp each eligible leg to zero. Preserve route and rate snapshot. Do not expose precise home addresses to ordinary approvers. | 55-20 plus 58-20 = 73 km; at 8 THB/km = 584 THB; office-to-customer has no commute deduction. |
| UX-13 | Automatically refreshed Google routes could rewrite history or violate provider retention terms. | Separate provider-derived transient routing data from the financial evidence retained under a verified license. A provider adapter must be disabled until credentials and storage rights are verified. Employee-attested manual distance is clearly labeled, never presented as Google verification. | Paid snapshots unchanged after policy/route updates; provider unavailable state is truthful. |
| UX-14 | A Trip with both expenses and an advance can be reimbursed twice. | One settlement allocates eligible approved actuals against previously paid advances. Only the net company top-up is payable, or an employee refund is due. Settlement is not complete until net cash movement is recorded and independently verified. | Both 20,000/17,600 and 20,000/22,000 scenarios; duplicate settlement/payment prevented. |
| UX-15 | 'Within 3 days' needs a visible due-date convention. | V1 due-date calculation uses trip-end local date plus 3 calendar days, with due-today lasting through the local day. No silent weekend extension. Make this policy explicit and versioned; owner can change future policy before live use. | Active, due, due-today, overdue and settled states tested at midnight boundaries. |
| UX-16 | Per diem rates and partial-day rules are unspecified. | Store policy values and calculation basis; no invented production rates. An unset rate blocks per-diem submission with an Admin action, not a zero-value success. Example rates are fixtures only. | Configuration-required state and frozen trip calculation preview. |
| UX-17 | Fake accounting integrations could make an unusable export look complete. | Easy-ACC and Smartbiz adapters remain disabled until a supported format is provided/verified. A neutral reconciliation export may be offered as such, never labeled a vendor import. Keep immutable job/input hash and retry receipt. | Unsupported adapter returns a specific configuration error; idempotency conflict rejects changed input. |
| UX-18 | Project master duplication causes inconsistent customer/project costs. | Local read-only ProjectReference projection keyed by source item ID. Show source and last sync, inactive/stale state; retain historical project snapshot. No local authoritative project-creation workflow. | Read-only integration contract and safe fixture tests; no production Microsoft write. |
| UX-19 | A finance queue with many unrelated cards reduces working density. | One working table, useful filters, right-aligned amounts, independently labeled states, a detail view/drawer, selection summary and explicit bulk confirmation. Totals must reflect filtered/selected rows and currency. | Realistic Thai rows at 1440 and tablet; keyboard navigation and horizontal table containment. |
| UX-20 | Generic dashboard aesthetics and accessibility omissions are likely. | No neon/glow/gradients/glass/oversized KPI or assistant boxes. Labels above controls, visible focus, text plus status color, minimum 44px mobile targets, 16px mobile inputs, readable Thai line height. Restrained 8/10/12px radius scale. | Actual rendered 390px, tablet and 1440px reviews, contrast/overflow checks and documented Taste + Astra critique. |

## Screen and state gaps to close

Employee: sign-in failure/unmapped employee, Home attention list, new chooser, leave balance/history, integer OT, all Expense subtypes, mileage legs, camera evidence, Trip/advance/settlement, My Requests with return-and-correct, immutable detail/history, profile. Head: inbox, detail, approve/return/reject with reasons, history, skipped self-request explanation. Finance: claims, verification, original receipts, advances, settlements, payment batches/detail, payroll cycles/items, accounting adapter state, reports, audit. Admin: employee mapping, departments/reporting lines, policy families and effective versions, holidays, expense types, approval rules, project integration and system readiness.

Every route must specify permissions, loading, empty, validation, error, retry, offline and success behavior. Tables must not be forced into unreadable full-width phone layouts. Employee workflows become stacked sections; Finance tables retain local horizontal scrolling with core identifying columns and a details action.

## Release-gating unknowns (not reasons to stop independent implementation)

Production Entra tenant/client/redirect and secret provisioning; real employee roster and salary/OT eligibility; company holiday calendar and policy amounts; supported Easy-ACC/Smartbiz format; SharePoint site/list and column mapping; Google API key and retention rights; production private object-storage configuration and backup destination; production hostname/TLS and explicit live cutover approval. Do not include credentials in this document, logs or commits.
